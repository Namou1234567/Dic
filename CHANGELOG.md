# Changelog

## 1.3.5 · 2026-11-28

- Repack the recordings in vocabulary order, 50 clips per file, and drop audio that no entry uses. The recordings shrink from 95 to 82 files and about 2 MB less, and flashcards in order rarely need a new file. Each phone downloads the recordings once more.
- The recording generator now repacks a language by itself when it uses more than two files beyond the minimum, so additions and removals no longer leave the files scattered. The checks enforce the same limit.

## 1.3.4 · 2026-11-28

- Lao recordings spell English abbreviations with Lao letter names, so ຕູ້ ATM is read as ຕູ້ ເອ ທີ ເອັມ instead of mixing in English. Six recordings are remade: ATM, VIP, SF6, GIS and two with UPS. The screen still shows the English letters.

## 1.3.3 · 2026-11-28

- Lao recordings no longer read English in brackets, which the Lao voice pronounced poorly, for example ກຳລັງໄຟຟ້າແທ້ (Active Power). The English is still shown. English outside brackets, such as ຕູ້ ATM, is still read. Ten recordings are remade.

## 1.3.2 · 2026-11-28

- Use one bracket style throughout: full-width （…） in Chinese meanings and a space before half-width (…) in Lao, for example 吗（疑问） and ກະບ່າຍ (ກະສວຍ). Wording is unchanged. Missed words saved under the old Lao spelling move to the updated entry.
- Apply a Lao colleague's review of words that shared a Chinese meaning. Keep one spelling each of lock (ແມ່ກະແຈ), tongue (ລີ້ນ) and policy (ນະໂຍບາຍ), and drop the extra wordings for safety helmet, justice, generator and transformer. Noun forms with ການ or ຄວາມ are labelled （名词）, for example 安全（名词） for ຄວາມປອດໄພ. Other meanings are made more precise: 计划（名词） and 做计划/规划, 肥皂粉, 树林/森林 and 油漆刷. Missed words under a removed wording move to the kept one. The vocabulary has 2,011 Lao headwords.

## 1.3.0 · 2026-11-27

- Mandarin recordings now use the Microsoft female voice Xiaoxiao at a slightly slower rate, so every word starts clearly. Lao recordings are unchanged.
- Words with several Chinese meanings show them on one line separated by slashes, for example 洗手间/厕所, with the pinyin in the same form. The Chinese button reads all meanings in one go with a short pause at each slash, so there is no longer a list to choose from. Repeated meanings such as 矮/低 and 低 are merged.
